Why It Matters

Your eyes impact every moment of your life.

Better eye health supports better daily functioning and independence — so you can keep doing what matters most.

👁
You Are Scared and Underserved

Most patients leave their ophthalmologist with "wait and see" and nothing else. No explanation of what is happening. No emotional support. No practical next steps. That gap is where Vitrenew lives.

🔬
The Research Gap Is Real

A systematic search across seven language traditions found no published non-surgical botanical protocol for vitreous hemorrhage clearance. The science exists. The documented application does not — yet.

🤝
You Deserve Better Guidance

Between watchful waiting and surgery, there is a gap. Vitrenew is building the education, community, and eventually the products to fill it — responsibly, with science, and with your independence as the goal.
